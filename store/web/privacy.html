Privacy Policy

Tasmee Hifz (com.tasmeehifz.app) · last updated 5 October 2026

Tasmee Hifz has no account system and no server of its own. The only personal data the app sends off your phone is your approximate location, and only when you open prayer times (see Location below). Everything else the app remembers about you is stored only on your phone, is left out of Android's cloud backup, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your recitation and the microphone

The app requests microphone access so it can follow your recitation on the page. Audio is passed to Android's own speech recognition service and is used only to decide which word you are reciting. Tasmee Hifz does not record audio to a file, does not keep audio after a session, and never uploads audio anywhere.

Speech recognition itself is provided by Android, not by this app. Google's recognizer can run fully on-device when the Arabic language pack is installed; some devices may otherwise fall back to a network-based recognition service supplied by the device manufacturer or by Google. That processing is governed by your device provider's privacy policy, not this one. Settings → Recognizer on this device shows whether on-device recognition is supported and whether the Arabic offline pack is installed, with a button to install it. Installing your phone's offline Arabic language pack keeps recognition entirely on the device.

Location

When you open prayer times, the app reads your device location to work out which prayer-time authority applies and to request the times for the days ahead. You can refuse the permission; the rest of the app works without it. It asks Android for approximate location only — never precise.

Two things outside this app see those coordinates, and you should know about both:

  • The public Aladhan prayer-time API (aladhan.com), which receives the latitude and longitude in order to return the times. No identifier is sent with them.
  • Android's own geocoder, which the app asks to turn the coordinates into a country and city name so the screen can tell you which authority it is following. On most Android phones that service is provided by Google, and the lookup happens under Android's privacy terms, not this app's.

Beyond that last cached result, this app stores no location and associates it with no identifier.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your reading position, session history, streak, and the memorization review schedule.
  • The list of words you missed or misread, used to build your personal error profile.
  • Your settings, including prayer-time corrections and the chosen reciter.
  • The most recent prayer-time response (including the coordinates it was requested for), kept until the next successful request replaces it, so the screen still shows something without a connection.

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your device. The app never transmits any of it, and it is excluded from Android's backup to your Google account. To move it to another phone, use Settings → Save a backup, which writes a file you keep yourself.

Network use

Four hosts, and nothing else:

  • api.aladhan.com — the prayer times for your coordinates, and the list of calculation authorities.
  • quranicaudio.com and download.quranicaudio.com — the reciter list, and the recitation audio itself, only when you press play in Listen.
  • Android's geocoder, as described under Location.

Everything else works with no network at all: the entire Quran text, all 604 mushaf pages, both hadith collections, the adhkar, the adhan playback and the recitation matching. Prayer times are the one screen that genuinely needs a connection — they are fetched, not calculated on the phone — so offline it shows the last saved times, labelled with the day they were saved, and the adhan stops sounding once those times are more than two days old.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, telemetry or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is transferred to anyone for their own purposes. The two exceptions above — Aladhan and Android's geocoder — receive coordinates solely to answer the request that needed them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone, children included, apart from the approximate location described under Location, used only to fetch prayer times.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here with its date.
