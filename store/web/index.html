An app that listens while you recite

Most Quran apps show you the page. This one watches you read it. Open any surah, start reciting, and the highlight moves across the printed mushaf with your voice. When you reach the last line, the page turns on its own.

You never tell it where you are. Begin in the middle of another surah, with or without the basmala, and it locates you across the whole Quran in about a second — because the app tracks a single position in a flat array of all 77,428 words of the Quran, not a position inside one surah.

Hidden mode: memorize with a page that does not move

Hidden mode blanks the page and reveals each word only when you say it. The geometry never shifts: the ghost of every hidden word holds its exact place, so the page you memorized looks the same whether the words are shown or hidden. A hafiz memorizes the shape of a page as much as its sound, and an app that reflows the line when a word appears has destroyed the thing being memorized.

Stuck on a word? Tap once for its first letter. Tap again for the whole word. Every hint is remembered, so the app knows which words you lean on.

Revision that knows what you are weak on

Each session grades the ayahs you recited from what actually happened — which words matched, which were missed, which needed a hint — and schedules them with spaced repetition. The tracker then shows these ayahs, today: weakest and most overdue first, grouped into contiguous passages you can genuinely recite, rather than shuffled single verses.

A personal error profile shows the words you confuse with each other. It is careful about blame in a way that matters: when the two words differ only by a sound the phone's Arabic recognizer cannot reliably hear, the app says so and points at your language settings, instead of telling you that you mispronounce a letter you pronounce correctly.

The real printed page

604 pages, 15 lines

Real Madani page boundaries and line breaks from the QPC V2 layout — including the eighteen surahs whose band closes a page with the text beginning overleaf. No computed layout produces that.

The mushaf's own typeface

Set in KFGQPC Uthmanic Script HAFS from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, bundled whole and unmodified, and checked on every build against all 73 codepoints the text uses.

Prayer times, adhan, hadith and adhkar

  • Prayer times by your country's own authority — Morocco, Egypt, Türkiye, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, Indonesia, Malaysia, France and more, matched automatically from where the phone is, with per-prayer minute corrections so the times match the mosque you actually follow.
  • The adhan as real audio, played in full with a stop button, not as a truncated notification chime — plus a five-minute warning that is structurally unable to carry the adhan sound.
  • Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim in full: 14,734 narrations, Arabic with English underneath, each with its collection and number so you can check it in print.
  • Morning and evening adhkar where not one Arabic character is typed by the app: every du'a is a verbatim passage from a bundled hadith or from the bundled mushaf.

What is free, and what is not

The Quran, the mushaf, the recitation follow-along, prayer times, the adhan, the adhkar and the hadith collections are free, and will stay free.

Worship does not belong behind a paywall. Every part of this app is free today, with no ads, no account and no tracking.

Privacy: nothing to sign up for, nothing collected

No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no profile. Your recitation is processed by the speech recognizer on your own device and is never recorded or uploaded by this app. The only two things that use the network at all are the prayer-time lookup and optional reciter audio; the entire Quran works in airplane mode. Full privacy policy.

Honest limits

  • Android only for now. There is no iOS build.
  • Arabic text only — no translation and no tafsir in this version.
  • The follow-along uses Android's own Arabic speech recognition. Install the offline Arabic language pack for the best results, and expect it to be less accurate than a human listener.
  • It does not grade tajweed or makharij. It follows which word you said, not how you said it.

Questions

Is there an app that listens to your Quran recitation?

Yes — that is what this one is for. Quran Habit tracks your voice through the mushaf word by word and marks the words you skip or misread, without needing to be told which ayah you are on.

Does it work without internet?

The Quran, all 604 pages, the hadith and the adhkar are bundled in the app. Prayer times are cached after the first fetch. Recitation following is fully offline once your phone has its Arabic language pack installed.

How does it help with hifz?

Hidden mode plus spaced revision scheduled from your real mistakes, so the ayahs you are actually weak on come back first.

Is it free?

Yes, and the Quran and worship features always will be. Only the coach layer is ever paid.
